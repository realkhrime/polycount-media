Shorts Publisher

Shorts Publisher is Polycount Media's own in-house tool. It uploads Polycount Media's own videos to Polycount Media's own YouTube channel and its own TikTok account, at the times the channel has set. It writes their titles and descriptions, and it reads the channel's own analytics so that every new video can be better than the last.

It is used only by Polycount Media, with its own accounts. It is not offered to anyone else, and nobody else can sign in to it.
