Privacy Policy

Shorts Publisher is a private tool run by Polycount Media (the owner). The owner uses it only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and the owner's own TikTok account (and, if connected later, the owner's own Facebook Page and Instagram account). It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.

What the tool can access

Only the permissions the owner approves when signing in to each platform:

  • YouTube: upload videos to the owner's channel, post comments on the owner's own videos, read the channel name and the YouTube Analytics reports of the owner's own videos, and search public videos (titles, tags, view counts) to see what is trending.
  • TikTok: read the owner's basic profile (open ID, avatar, display name) and posting settings (user.info.basic), upload the owner's videos to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts (video.upload), post the owner's videos directly to the owner's own TikTok profile (video.publish), and read their publishing status.
  • Facebook and Instagram (only if connected): publish the owner's videos to the owner's own accounts and read their publishing status.

What it stores, and where

Login tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. A record of what was posted (video IDs, links, titles, captions), saved public trend data and the owner's own analytics are kept in the owner's project folder. Nothing is sent anywhere except to the platforms' own official APIs.

What it does not do

It does not collect personal information about other people beyond public video statistics. It does not sell, rent or share any data, and it does not use data for advertising, profiling or tracking.

Use of Google user data

Shorts Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data deletion

Deleting the folder .shorts-publisher in the owner's user profile on the owner's computer (and the older %LOCALAPPDATA%\ShortsPublisher, if present) removes all login tokens. Access can be revoked at any time in each platform's settings:

  • Google: Google Account > Security > Your connections to third-party apps and services
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions
  • Facebook: Settings > Business integrations

Questions or deletion requests: contact Polycount Media through its YouTube channel, https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC0A_usrHhIPSbZ5IJX7k54w.
